LATENT MAGNET V2 / READ WITH model.txt AND proof.json

model.txt is the executable estimation source, included WHOLE. proof.json holds
all 16 ablations and full held-out ledgers: [slug, actual cap, v1, v2, P25, P75].
No source or model evidence is omitted. The baseline is frozen before these tests.

forecast(): TVL times category/global clipped median times retained growth,
maturity and completeness. Mask 15 retains all four original v1 terms. Every
term's removal is evaluated on both splits; no change may worsen either median.
The guard retains v1 when no ablation improves both. Selection reuses validation;
it is not an independent test. Common token IDs can duplicate token economics.

validateModel(): remove the target, refit peers, compare with its recorded cap.
Walk-forward uses strictly earlier listing cohorts, minimum 24 priced records;
unknown dates are excluded. All features are from ONE snapshot, so listing order
is not a historical backtest. Median x-error = median(max(pred/actual,actual/pred)).
dispersion(): raw peer P25/P75, category n>=5 else pooled, times signal factors.
It is dispersion, not calibrated confidence; point can fall outside its band.
Rank envelopes compare interval bounds, not a confidence set. Nearest peers use
absolute log TVL distance; all pool records, not only the nine shown, set value.

fitOdds(): positives are priced records; controls still record no token; unknown
price outcomes are excluded. Priors, shrinkage strength and age cuts are learned
from snapshot counts. This prevalence proxy has censoring and survivor bias;
eventual launch odds and horizon hazards are unidentifiable from one snapshot.

economicCase(): reward = odds * implied cap * assumed wallet allocation. Allocation,
fees, 5% opportunity APR, 1% capital risk charge and $15/hour are scenarios. Central
share is 1 ppm at $100; scale min(pilot/100,2). 1 ppm = 0.0001%. Horizon affects
costs only. Principal is assumed returned, but full loss and zero rewards are
possible. Net charges 4 fee actions, capital costs and time. FARM requires net>0
and >=$15/hour surplus AFTER the $15/hour time charge; positive lower net is
MARGINAL; else SKIP. Eligibility, integrations and distributions are unverified.
No token recorded is not proof of no token. These are entry estimates, not FDV.
